Cleaning services for every kind of home

Every home is different, so every quote is built around your space, your schedule and your priorities. Here is exactly what each service covers, who it suits best, and how to get the most from it.

Standard cleaning

Standard cleaning is the dependable rhythm that keeps a lived-in home under control. It covers the surfaces and rooms that get used and dirtied every day, so the home always feels ready for guests, work or rest.

Most clients book standard cleaning on a weekly, every-two-weeks or monthly schedule. Regular visits are easier on the home than occasional heavy cleaning, because dirt never has time to build up into something stubborn.

Best for: families, professionals and anyone who wants a consistently tidy home.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop wiped and sanitized
  • Exterior of appliances cleaned
  • Bathrooms scrubbed: toilet, tub, shower, sink and mirror
  • Dusting of reachable surfaces and furniture
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped to suit the material
  • Trash emptied and liners replaced
  • Beds made on request

Deep cleaning

Deep cleaning goes beyond the daily surface. We spend more time on the places that standard cleaning does not reach: baseboards, door frames, the inside of appliances and the grime that settles in corners over months.

It is the ideal first visit for a new client, because it brings the home to a clean baseline that regular visits can then maintain. Many clients also book a deep clean each spring and autumn, before the holidays or ahead of hosting an event.

Best for: first-time visits, seasonal resets, homes that have gone a while without a thorough clean.

  • Everything in a standard cleaning
  • Baseboards, door frames and light switches
  • Cabinet fronts and handles
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Detailed scrubbing of bathroom grout and fixtures
  • Behind and under easy-to-move furniture
  • Ceiling fans and vents dusted

Move-in / move-out cleaning

A move is stressful enough. Move-in and move-out cleaning takes one job off your list and makes sure an empty home is genuinely clean, whether you are handing back a rental, preparing to sell or arriving at a new place.

Because the home is empty, we can reach every cabinet, drawer, closet and appliance interior. That level of access means a result that is hard to match in an occupied home.

Best for: tenants, landlords, sellers, buyers and property managers.

  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Inside oven, fridge and dishwasher
  • Walls spot-cleaned and fixtures wiped
  • Bathrooms and kitchen detailed from top to bottom
  • Floors cleaned throughout
  • Windows and sills wiped

Short-term rental turnover

For hosts, the cleaning between guests is part of the product. A missed hair in the shower or a dusty shelf shows up in the next review. Our turnover service is built around speed and consistency, so each guest arrives to the same standard.

Tell us your checkout and check-in times, how you handle linens and supplies, and what your best reviews say guests love. We will build the checklist around that.

Best for: Airbnb and Vrbo hosts, vacation rental owners and property managers.

  • Full bathroom and kitchen reset
  • Beds stripped and remade with fresh linens
  • Surfaces dusted and wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and supplies checked
  • Notes on damage or items left behind

Post-renovation cleaning

Construction leaves behind fine dust that settles on every surface, plus stickers, residue and debris. Post-renovation cleaning clears it all, so the space you invested in actually looks the way you imagined.

These jobs vary a great deal in size, so we ask for photos or a short description before quoting. We may suggest more than one visit for larger projects.

Best for: homeowners finishing a remodel, contractors handing over a finished job.

  • Construction dust removed from surfaces and fixtures
  • Cabinets cleaned inside and out
  • Windows, sills and tracks wiped
  • Stickers, tape and residue removed where safe
  • Floors cleaned to suit the new material
  • Bathrooms and kitchen detailed

Optional extras

Add any of these to a visit when you request your quote. They are quoted along with the main service.

  • Inside the oven
  • Inside the fridge
  • Interior windows
  • Laundry: wash, dry and fold

How we price

We do not publish fixed prices because the right number depends on your home. We look at the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the condition of the home, the service you choose, how often you want visits, and any extras. Recurring clients generally pay less per visit than one-time clients, because a home we already know takes less time to restore.

When you send a quote request we reply with a clear price. There is no obligation to book.
