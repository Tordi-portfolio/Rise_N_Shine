What a visit looks like

Every home is cleaned in a deliberate order: top to bottom, back to front, and dry work before wet work. Dust falls before we vacuum. Surfaces are wiped before floors are mopped. It is a simple discipline, and it is the difference between a home that looks clean for an hour and one that stays clean for days.

In the kitchen we work on counters, the sink, the stovetop, the outside of appliances and the surfaces your family touches most. In bathrooms we scrub and sanitize toilets, showers, tubs, sinks and mirrors, and we pay attention to grout lines and fixtures where buildup hides.

Living areas and bedrooms get dusted from high surfaces down, with floors vacuumed or mopped to suit the material. We respect your belongings: we move small items to clean beneath them and return them where they were.

Before we leave, we walk through the home against the agreed checklist, so you are not left guessing what was done. If you would like a specific task done differently, say so once and we’ll note it for every future visit.

Tell us if you prefer fragrance-free products, if a family member has sensitivities, or if there are rooms we should skip. A good cleaning relationship is built on small, clear instructions.
